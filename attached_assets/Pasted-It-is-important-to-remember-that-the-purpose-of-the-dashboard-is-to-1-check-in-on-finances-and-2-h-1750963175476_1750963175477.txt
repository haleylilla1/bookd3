It is important to remember that the purpose of the dashboard is to 1) check in on finances and 2) have a clean report to be able to transfer to a CPA at the end of the year. Note - all the data in the following serves as just an example and should not be actually used as gig info.

📄 PDF REPORT STRUCTURE
Purpose: CPA-ready reports for freelancers to summarize income, expenses, and mileage with image documentation.

1. 🧾 COVER PAGE
* Business Name / User Name 
* Reporting Period (e.g. "January 2025" or "2025 Annual Report") 
* Date Generated 
* Contact Info (Email, Phone) 
* Optional: Logo or profile image 

2. 💰 INCOME SUMMARY
Section Title: “Income Summary”
Date	Source	Gig Type	Amount

01/25/25	Direct Client	Photo shoot	$650.00
...	...	...	...
TOTAL			$1,850.00
* Subtotal at bottom 
* Include monthly subtotal if generating an annual report

5. 📷 RECEIPT IMAGE ATTACHMENTS
Section Title: “Receipt Archive”
* Receipts organized by date 
* Optional: small caption with expense description and amount 
* Show thumbnails or embed full image depending on report size 
* Display as grid or 1 per page
