Original Features of Giggy:

Working Title: Giggy
Tagline (optional): Work different.
Goal of the App:
To become the #1 companion app for live-service gig workers—like brand ambassadors, freelance catering staff, and bartenders—by simplifying gig tracking, payment monitoring, tax prep, and income goal setting.
Target Users:
Freelance workers in events—brand ambassadors, catering staff, bartenders—who need to log spontaneous, varied income with minimal friction and no missed details.
Core Value Prop:
Unlike other gig or budgeting apps, Giggy is purpose-built for live event workers. It lets users:
Log gigs in seconds
Track pay, expenses, and mileage
Prep for taxes
Auto-generate resume data
Visualize earnings and progress toward financial goals
📱 SCREEN 1: CALENDAR VIEW
Title: Calendar
Monthly calendar grid
Colored dots on dates with gigs
"+ Add Gig" button on bottom right
📝 SCREEN 2: GIG ENTRY FORM
Title: New Gig Entry
Fields:
Type of Gig (dropdown or create your own)
Client Name (auto-complete from saved)
Date (pre-filled if clicked from calendar)
Expected Amount
Actual Paid (Yes/No + field for $)
Payment Method (dropdown)
Expenses (Yes/No + categories: Parking, Clothes, etc.)
Duties (text field for resume)
Suggested Tax % (default to 23%)
Mileage (X to Y or multi-stop)
Long Distance? (Yes/No, enter flights, Ubers)
Notes
Save Button
📊 SCREEN 3: DASHBOARD
Title: Dashboard
Sections:
Weekly Income
Monthly Income
Annual Income
Top Clients (Leaderboard)
Total Gigs Completed
Suggested Tax Withheld
Goal Progress (ex: $2,500/$3,000 this month)
📄 SCREEN 4: RESUME BUILDER
Title: My Resume
List of gigs (with dates, client, duties) in a nice professional resume template
Toggle each gig: Include/Exclude
"Export to PDF" button
🎯 SCREEN 5: GIG-TO-GOAL TRACKER
Title: Allocate Your Earnings
Show recent gig: $500
Allocation:
$200 to Savings ✅
$200 to Rent ✅
$100 unassigned ➡ suggest category
Visual: Progress bars for each goal
💡 Keep it SUPER SIMPLE.
Each entry should take less than 1 minute
Autofill fields where possible
Option to sync with bank account for payment confirmation
End results:
Gig workers have a gig working calendar to manage booking.
Gig workers can log each gig, mileage, expenses, and reimbursements.
Gig workers can see a total list view of all the gigs they worked, how much was made from each gig, and how much to expect to take out for taxes.
Gig workers can export their information in a way that is readable for a CPA. (So maybe google sheet to start)
